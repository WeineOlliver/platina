    Roadmap

    Stage 1: Complete the Main Story on 'Death March!' Difficulty

    If you are new to the Witcher series, then it is highly recommended that you not to play on Death March difficulty first, it's suggested that you play through the game on a lower difficulty for your first playthrough. Once you feel confident with most of the enemies in the game such as their fighting techniques and know how to control Geralt confidently then you may start this stage. Although this will take you longer to achieve your platinum, you will feel less stressed when it comes to playing on Death March difficulty.

    During this stage your main focus is to complete the main story on Death March difficulty. Keep in mind that on this difficulty your heath does not recover by mediating. You must keep in mind that food, water, etc. is the only way to heal your health. It's highly recommended that you do as many side missions and witcher contracts as possible as you will be under-leveled for some main story missions. You can also go to undiscovered locations to collect new missions and find loot to make the hard missions easier. There are a few missable trophies in this game, so try and get some of them during this playthrough to have less pressure on the second playthrough.

    Getting all Missable Trophies

    There are a number of missable trophies in The Witcher 3 that can be missed if you aren't paying attention to them. Missing these can be avoided with some preparation beforehand. This section will attempt to give you a quick breakdown on when to work on missable trophies:
    •  Card Collector - If you're interested in collecting all gwent cards in the game, you'll pretty much need to pay attention from the start of the game, there are many opportunities throughout the game where collecting a card is missable.
    •  Friends with Benefits - If you don't complete this set of sidequests by the time you are close to the end of the games main story, it is missable. You also need to romance her and then send her to Kaer Morhen during her final sidequest to avoid missing the Full Crew Trophy.
    •  Kingmaker - Once you get to Skellige Islands and complete the quest "The King is Dead - Long Live the King", the secondary quests related to this trophy all open up, you need to complete these quests before finishing the main story quest called "Brothers in Arms" or else this trophy will become missable.
    •  Full Crew - This trophy requires you to have a number of missable required characters to be recruitable for a quest near the end of the game. You will want to do the Keira Metz subplot, the Triss Merigold subplot, along with the subplot regarding Roche, Ves, & Zolten before completing the "Brothers In Arms" quest near the end of the game. Some of these subquests require you to do specific things while playing the quests, so be sure to take a look at this trophy and read up on the specifics.
    •  Gwent Master - This trophy is missable if you lose a round in the qwent tournament, you will be knocked out completely and will no longer be able to replay the tournament unless you reload to a previous save. It's highly suggested that after you defeat each opponent, you create a manual save so you can revert back in case you lose.
    •  Woodland Spirit - If you choose the wrong dialogue options during this secondary quest/"contract", then the trophy becomes missable. Take a look at that trophy for more information.
    •  Even Odds - Be sure to kill 2 monsters that you have a contract on without using signs, potions, mutagens, oils, or bombs. If you complete all contracts before getting this trophy, then you'll have to start a new game to get it.

    Make a save file before getting on a boat and sailing to the Isle of Mists. There is a quest near the end of the games main storyline called The Isle of Mists, this is a crucial point in the story. It is highly recommended that you create a manual save file before getting on a boat and sailing to the Isle of Mists as the following trophies will become missable if you don't earn these trophies and/or perform certain requirements for them:
    •  Assassin of Kings - You need to complete five quests in this subplot before venturing out to the Isle of Mists. You also need to complete all of the Triss Merigold subquests.
    •  Fast and Furious - Once you reach the Isle of Mists, the quest giver for the Velen horse racers will not be interested in giving you any races to do, thus not allowing you to get this trophy at that point.
    •  Geralt: The Professional - If you did not complete the Devil by the Well witcher contract in White Orchard by now, then that quest will automatically fail once you've reached the Isle of Mists, making this trophy unobtainable (This trophy has been patched with update 1.07 so Devil by the Well isn't required for the trophy).

    New Game Plus Mode

    As part of a recent patch for the game, New Game Plus mode is now available as free add-on content on the PlayStation Store. With the latest patch installed, you can start New Game Plus mode after finishing the game once and your player experience will be carried over if it is above level 30. If you finish the game below level 30, then you will automatically start at level 30 on New Game Plus. You will also be able to select any difficulty level, making this trophy obtainable via New Game Plus Mode.

    The items you will be able to keep is armor, weapons, money, food, and alchemy recipes, any other items (like quest items) will not be kept. It should be noted that Gwent Cards will not be carried over to New Game Plus Mode. In addition, enemies will be a lot stronger (all at least level 30) in New Game Plus mode.

    With this in mind, you will be able to utilize the in-game glitches listed below via playing the game to get money along with leveling up Geralt to a desired level (It's recommended you level him to about level 65 or higher using the level-up glitches, then start New Game Plus as enemies start out at level 30 minimum on New Game Plus). Then you will be able to update the game to its most current version and then start the game on New Game Plus mode, carrying over your level, money, and so on and then finish the game from there and obtain this trophy.

    So, before starting New Game Plus Mode you will want to do the following on your normal story-finished game save. You will want to create or have Superior Swallow Potion in your inventory as those rapidly restore your health and carry over as an item to New Game Plus Mode. You'll also want to stock up on as many Alcohest as you feel you need (Recommended that you stock up on 99 of them) as when you meditate on Death March difficulty, Alcohest replenishes your Swallow Potions. You'll likely have to create your swallow potions via alchemy by first making a Swallow Potion, then an Enhanced Swallow Potion, then a Superior Swallow Potion. Alcohest can be bought from many alchemy salespeople. Then get your level to a recommended 65 or above by using the glitches detailed below, stock up on around 50,000 coins, find some good weapons, armor, and some food just in case you run out of Superior Swallow in a battle and can't meditate. Make sure you're all set, then you can go on to start a fairly easy New Game Plus Mode playthrough. While on New Game Plus, you can still utilize XP, money, and even the secondary "god mode" glitch detailed below to go even further if needed.

This section is mostly for the players who want to blaze through their "Death March!" difficulty playthrough with as little frustration and with as much efficiency as possible.     A note about not updating the game or deleting update data: After 12 updates, the developers have patched out some of the useful glitches in this game such as the unlimited money glitches along with the "God Mode" glitches. It used to not be necessary to delete your update data, but now it's up to you to decide. If you go the New Game Plus route, you'll have to stay updated to your current version of the game. If you want to not use New Game Plus mode and perfer to take advantage of all the glitches, you should delete your update data once you've gotten all other trophies and are ready to start a new playthrough. To delete update data, you'll have to delete the game. Remember to have all of your saved data backed up! The following is what you do once you're back on the unpatched version of the game and how to stop it from updating:
    • When you launch the game, it will attempt to download an update to the game, you will want to go to the PS4's download area under "Notifications" and pause it by highlighting the download and pressing  over it. You will have to go there and pause the download every time you launch the game and every time there is a new update available for the game. 
    • If the download for the update file is getting close to finishing, you can highlight the download and press the "Options" button on your controller and delete it, then the update will have to start over again.
    • For people who have already updated their game but want to play the unpatched version of the game, you'll have to backup your save file using PSN's cloud save feature (requires PlayStation Plus) or use a USB drive to backup your save file, then delete your game and re-install it where you will want to deny game updates once they try to download.
